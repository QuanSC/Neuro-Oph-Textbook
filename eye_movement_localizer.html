How to read the map

The brainstem is drawn as three floors: midbrain on top, pons in the middle, medulla at the bottom. Left and right sides are split by the dotted midline.

Purple bosses decide where the eyes go. The teal phone line (MLF) links the two sides. Amber workers carry the order to each eye.
