Clinical Activity Score

Check each feature present. A score of 3 or more out of 7 indicates active thyroid eye disease.

0of 7: inactive
Activity vs severity
Activity (CAS) tells you whether inflammation is ongoing; severity (mild, moderate to severe, sight-threatening) tells you how much damage it is causing. Treatment depends on both.
Treatment framework
All patients: stop smoking, restore euthyroidism, lubricate. Moderate to severe active disease: IV glucocorticoids or teprotumumab are common first-line choices; other options include mycophenolate, tocilizumab, rituximab and orbital radiotherapy. Inactive disease: rehabilitative surgery in order: decompression, then strabismus, then lids.
Teprotumumab (OPTIC trial)
83% had a proptosis response of 2 mm or more at week 24 vs 10% with placebo. The FDA label warns of hearing impairment that may be permanent: test hearing before, during and after treatment. Hyperglycemia and inflammatory bowel disease flares are other warnings.
Sight-threatening
Dysthyroid optic neuropathy (reduced acuity or color, RAPD, field loss, apical crowding) or corneal breakdown: IV methylprednisolone and urgent orbital decompression if no rapid response.

Where the numbers come from

  • OPTIC: proptosis response of 2 mm or more at week 24 in 83% with teprotumumab vs 10% with placebo (83 patients): Douglas RS, Kahaly GJ, Patel A, et al. Teprotumumab for the treatment of active thyroid eye disease. N Engl J Med. 2020;382(4):341-352. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1910434.
  • Clinical Activity Score ranges 0 to 7; a score of 3 or more indicates active disease: as defined in Smith TJ, Kahaly GJ, Ezra DG, et al. Teprotumumab for thyroid-associated ophthalmopathy. N Engl J Med. 2017;376(18):1748-1761.
  • Teprotumumab hearing warning: US Food and Drug Administration. TEPEZZA prescribing information, revised July 2023 (accessdata.fda.gov): severe hearing impairment including hearing loss, in some cases permanent; assess hearing before, during and after treatment.
  • MOGAD and NMOSD criteria: Banwell B, et al. Lancet Neurol. 2023;22(3):268-282. Wingerchuk DM, et al. Neurology. 2015;85(2):177-189.
  • Orbit diagrams are schematic. Other content reflects standard teaching in the core texts and curriculum readings.
