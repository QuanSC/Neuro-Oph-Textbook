Screening tests (2025 revision)
OCT is the primary tool, with wide-pattern fundus autofluorescence. Visual fields (10-2 for parafoveal disease; 24-2, 30-2 or 24-2C to include pericentral disease) and multifocal ERG are confirmatory. Baseline OCT and FAF near the start of therapy.
Patterns
Parafoveal damage is typical in patients of European heritage; pericentral damage near the arcades is common in East Asian patients. Screen for both.
Remember
Toxicity is generally not reversible. Mild retinopathy is unlikely to progress after HCQ is stopped, but severe retinopathy may keep worsening for years, which is why early detection matters. Confirm subtle findings with a second test before stopping a valuable medication, and communicate with the prescriber.

Where the numbers come from

  • HCQ: keep daily dose at or under 5.0 mg/kg real weight; at that dose, toxicity risk is under 1% at 5 years and under 2% at 10 years, rising to almost 20% after 20 years; major risks are high dose, long duration, kidney disease and tamoxifen; annual screening after 5 years if low risk; wider field patterns for Asian patients: Marmor MF, Kellner U, Lai TYY, Mieler WF; American Academy of Ophthalmology. Recommendations on screening for chloroquine and hydroxychloroquine retinopathy (2016 revision). Ophthalmology. 2016;123(6):1386-1394.
  • 2025 revision: dose unchanged at 5.0 mg/kg real weight or less, under 400 mg/day in severe obesity; OCT plus wide-pattern FAF as primary tests, fields and mfERG as confirmatory; baseline OCT and FAF near initiation; older age at initiation as an added risk; 24-2C fields can cover parafoveal and pericentral regions: Marmor MF, Ahn SJ, Ehlers JP, Melles RB, Mieler WF, Sarraf D, Yusuf IH; American Academy of Ophthalmology. Special AAO report: recommendations on screening for hydroxychloroquine retinopathy (2025 revision). Ophthalmology. 2026;133(4):439-450. doi:10.1016/j.ophtha.2025.11.001.
  • Other content reflects standard teaching in the core texts; dosing thresholds for other drugs are deliberately left qualitative. Verify against current drug labels.
