Sort dizziness by timing and triggers

The quality of dizziness (spinning vs lightheaded) is unreliable; timing and triggers are what localize. Pick a pattern.

Right posterior canal BPPV: Dix-Hallpike and Epley

Step through the maneuver. The left panel shows the patient's position; the right panel shows the otoconia (dot) moving through the right posterior canal toward the utricle.

Horizontal canal BPPV
Tested with the supine roll test. Geotropic nystagmus (beating toward the ground) on both sides suggests canalithiasis, with the affected ear on the side of stronger nystagmus. Apogeotropic nystagmus suggests cupulolithiasis or debris near the cupula, with the affected ear on the side of weaker nystagmus. Treat with a barbecue roll or Gufoni maneuver.
When it isn't BPPV
Positional nystagmus without latency, that doesn't fatigue, is purely vertical (downbeat) or doesn't match a canal pattern suggests central positional nystagmus: image the posterior fossa.

Where the numbers come from

  • Vestibular migraine: at least 5 episodes of moderate or severe vestibular symptoms lasting 5 minutes to 72 hours; current or past migraine; migraine features with at least 50% of episodes; not better explained otherwise: Lempert T, Olesen J, Furman J, et al. Vestibular migraine: diagnostic criteria (update). J Vestib Res. 2022;32(1):1-6. doi:10.3233/VES-201644.
  • HINTS performance in acute vestibular syndrome: Kattah JC, Talkad AV, Wang DZ, Hsieh YH, Newman-Toker DE. Stroke. 2009;40(11):3504-3510.
  • Definite Ménière disease: two or more spontaneous vertigo episodes of 20 minutes to 12 hours, audiometric low- to medium-frequency sensorineural hearing loss in the affected ear, fluctuating aural symptoms, not better explained (probable: 20 minutes to 24 hours without audiometric proof): Lopez-Escamez JA, Carey J, Chung WH, et al. Diagnostic criteria for Menière's disease. J Vestib Res. 2015;25(1):1-7. doi:10.3233/VES-150549.
  • PPPD: dizziness, unsteadiness or non-spinning vertigo on most days for 3 months or more, worse with upright posture, movement and complex visual stimuli; a chronic functional vestibular disorder, not structural or psychiatric: Staab JP, Eckhardt-Henn A, Horii A, et al. Diagnostic criteria for persistent postural-perceptual dizziness (PPPD). J Vestib Res. 2017;27(4):191-208. doi:10.3233/VES-170622.
  • Timing-and-triggers approach: based on the TiTrATE framework described by Newman-Toker and Edlow.
  • Maneuver diagrams are schematic. Other content reflects standard teaching.
