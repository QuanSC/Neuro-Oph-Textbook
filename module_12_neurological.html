Where the numbers come from

  • Ice pack test: 16 of 20 (80%) patients with myasthenic ptosis improved by 2 mm or more after 2 minutes of ice: Golnik KC, Pena R, Lee AG, Eggenberger ER. An ice test for the diagnosis of myasthenia gravis. Ophthalmology. 1999;106(7):1282-1286. Later studies report variable sensitivity, especially with mild ptosis, and higher yield with repeated testing (Park JY, Yang HK, Hwang JM. PLoS One. 2017;12:e0177078).
  • Thymectomy plus prednisone improved Quantitative MG score and lowered prednisone requirements over 3 years vs prednisone alone in generalized, nonthymomatous, AChR-positive MG (ages 18 to 65): Wolfe GI, Kaminski HJ, Aban IB, et al. Randomized trial of thymectomy in myasthenia gravis. N Engl J Med. 2016;375(6):511-522.
  • Simulated lid positions are illustrative. Other content reflects standard teaching in the core texts and curriculum readings.
