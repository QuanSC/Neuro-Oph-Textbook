Where the numbers come from

  • Causes of 904 homonymous hemianopias: stroke 69.6%, trauma 13.6%, tumor 11.3%; lesions most often occipital (45%) and optic radiations (32.2%); every type of homonymous defect except temporal crescent loss and sectoranopia occurred at all retrochiasmal locations: Zhang X, Kedar S, Lynn MJ, Newman NJ, Biousse V. Homonymous hemianopias: clinical-anatomic correlations in 904 cases. Neurology. 2006;66(6):906-910. doi:10.1212/01.wnl.0000203913.12088.93.
  • Spontaneous recovery in 38.4% of 263 homonymous hemianopias, less likely the longer the time from injury: Zhang X, Kedar S, Lynn MJ, Newman NJ, Biousse V. Natural history of homonymous hemianopia. Neurology. 2006;66(6):901-905.
  • Congruency and localization: Kedar S, Zhang X, Lynn MJ, Newman NJ, Biousse V. Congruency in homonymous hemianopia. Am J Ophthalmol. 2007. doi:10.1016/j.ajo.2007.01.048.
  • Other content reflects standard teaching in the core texts; verify specifics against them.
